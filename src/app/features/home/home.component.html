Background & Philosophy

Focus on ERP & Business Applications

Building stable, transactional backend architectures and role-governed systems.

My core development experience is centered around ERP and enterprise business applications. Rather than building transient web experiences, my day-to-day engineering has involved software that runs day-to-day operations for businesses — managing mission-critical transactional lifecycles from sales leads to warehouse fulfillment.

I specialize in database-backed architectures utilizing ASP.NET, C#, SQL Server, Entity Framework, and ADO.NET on the backend, complemented by clean, functional client-side development with JavaScript and modern Angular.

My work prioritizes data integrity, schema consistency, clear authorization boundaries, and maintainable business logic.
